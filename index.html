ABOUT CODEVIBE

More Than a Showcase.

CODEVIBE is a one-day AI startup and product showcase where student teams and early-stage founders present working prototypes and MVPs to a panel of industry experts and investors.

Show what you’ve built, get expert feedback, and explore opportunities to take your product further.

03 — THE CODEVIBE JOURNEYFROM PROTOTYPE TO POSSIBILITY

A DAY BUILT AROUND YOUR PRODUCT

Show the work.
Find what’s next.

A path to showcase, validate and take your product to the next level.

  1. 01▤

    Registration & screening

    Submit your product details. Selected teams will be invited to participate.

  2. 02▱

    Product showcase

    Set up your booth and demonstrate your MVP to judges and attendees.

  3. 03◎

    Expert evaluation

    Get feedback from industry experts on your product, market potential and execution feasibility.

  4. 04✳

    Finalist selection

    Top teams may be shortlisted for the investor pitch round based on evaluation criteria.

  5. 05↗

    Investor pitch opportunity

    Selected finalists get the opportunity to pitch their ventures to investors.

* Investor pitches are an opportunity for selected finalists, not a guaranteed part of every team's journey.
